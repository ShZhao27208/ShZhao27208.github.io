### 👋 About Me

I'm an undergraduate researcher in Professor **Wang Feng**'s group, working on **inorganic long-persistence luminescent materials**. My work spans phosphor synthesis, luminescence mechanism analysis, and AI-powered research tools.

- 🔬 **Research**: Rare-earth doped phosphors (Ce³⁺, Tb³⁺, Sm³⁺, Eu³⁺), color tuning, thermal stability
- 🛠 **Skills**: XRD, fluorescence spectroscopy, Origin, Materials Studio
- 🤖 **Building**: Open-source AI tools for scientific research workflows
- 🏆 **Awards**: 5 national-level competition awards, 3 university honors
- ✉️ **Contact**: zhaoshuomk@163.com · WeChat: zs2720828562
