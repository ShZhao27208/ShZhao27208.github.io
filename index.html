Zhao Shuo, male, from Xi'an, Shaanxi Province, a 2022 undergraduate student majoring in Materials Science and Engineering at Chongqing Jiaotong University. In August 2023, joined Professor Wang Feng's research group, focusing on inorganic long-persistence luminescent materials. Proficient in operating equipment such as XRD and fluorescence spectrometers, as well as scientific research software including Origin and Materials Studio. Research projects include: Sr₁.₅Ca₀.₅Al₂SiO₇:aCe⁴⁺,Tb³⁺ (CrystEngComm, first author, 1/9); BaCa₂Y₆O₁₂:Sm³⁺ (Ceramics International, co-first author, second position, 2/7); CaY₂Ge₄O₁₂:Eu³⁺ (CrystEngComm, co-first author, second position, 2/7); YAlGeO₅:Sm³⁺ (Ceramics International, third author, 3/10). Also a core member of the municipal-level innovation project "Nanoscale Confinement Regulation of Vanadium-based Multicomponent Metal-organic Frameworks and Research on Aqueous Zinc-ion Battery Energy Storage" (second in rank). Received five awards from national and other levels of competitions, and three honors and scholarships at the school level.Served as a student assistant for the metallography competition, coordinating events for over 200 participants, and held the position of class study committee member for three years and calligraphy club president for one year. Possesses a solid professional foundation and strong self-learning ability, capable of adapting to high-intensity work environments, with good teamwork and communication skills.
